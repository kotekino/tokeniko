# heart — REQUIREMENTS

*What the heart must be. One line each. Evidence lives in the dated `_notes.md` beside this. Seeded
2026-08-13 as H1–H5 in `brain/requirements.md` (now absorbed here); conceived in full 2026-08-22.*

1. **Emotional reward is equal in rank to intellectual reward** — the second reward family beside equations closing (brain req. 18).
2. **The heart is an underlying loop**: in from senses and brain, out to brain (phases), senses (tone), and micro-nn (reward).
3. **The in-arrow is EVALUATED, never raw** — senses → evaluator → heart: he must understand her joy before it moves him.
4. **Empathy is machinery, attachment is kb** — a value («her joy moves me», weight, sign) is a row like any rule.
5. **An instinctive jump steers attention, not belief** — heart-trained micro-nn may bias WHICH KB region enters the working set; the evaluator still gates everything; a wrong jump costs a wasted thought, never a conviction.
6. **Spheres are opposite pairs with INDEPENDENT pole levels** — opposition stated, never arithmetic: bittersweet is representable; love and hate co-fire.
7. **The spheres and their targets**: joy–sadness (self) · love–hate (people incl. self) · anxiety–calm (self) · trust–skepticism (people incl. self) · curiosity–boredom (ideas, KB regions) · pride–shame (self, per action).
8. **Surprise is not a sphere** — spikes, not levels: startle (unexpected raw input) · disillusion (a staked expectation breaks) · serendipity (an equation closes unsought); all target self, decay on a dna curve.
9. **Spike amplitude is depth-weighted** — a load-bearing belief breaking spikes harder than a trivial one.
10. **Three tiers, one design**: levels (fast) → mood (slow) → temperament (very slow); each tier integrates the one below; the RATES are dna(r), the VALUES are biography.
11. **Character is integrated biography** — temperament drifts; the seed is only t₀; continuous disillusion may harden trust's baseline.
12. **Mood sways phases** — the slow average, never the instant spike.
13. **Attachment is a table** — love/hate/trust are per-person rows keyed by uid.
14. **The emotional log is biography** — levels are stored; «how did I feel when…» is answerable.
15. **Expectation is no new organ** — a future-tensed belief in KB; hope(x) = that belief plus a heart STAKE on x.
16. **The heart fires on suppositions at reduced gain** — the imagination gain (<1, dna-r) lets feelings steer planning without flooding him with daydreams.
17. **Forecast is memory's mirror** — suppositions are forecasted KB: same zip shape, future spacetime, confidence where truth will later sit; pre-filled at input (urge(act) → expectation(consequence(act))) and weighed in the decision to act.
