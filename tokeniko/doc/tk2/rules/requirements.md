# rules — REQUIREMENTS

*What the rule layer must respect. One line each. Evidence lives in the dated `_notes.md` beside this.*

1. **A rule is a zip** — seed or experience, same shape as a fact, same machinery.
2. **Solvable ⇒ the rule carries the action. Unsolvable ⇒ the action is the SEARCH.**
3. **Many solutions ⇒ a choice**, collapsed by urge.
4. **Prohibition is not a second axis** — it is another rule's solution. Possible and permitted are one axis.
5. **Moral obligation EMERGES** from a conflicting KB; it is never a designed subsystem.
6. **Conflict resolves by defeat IN CONTEXT, never deletion** — defeasible and scoped.
7. **An open conflict is a legal state.** Unresolved obligation is conscience, not an error.
8. **Exactly three hardwired action categories**: `say[channel, user|null]` · `swapPhase()` · `commit`.
9. **Verbs select the act; the category is the socket.** The three categories are the only hardwired semantics.
10. **Capability ≠ understanding.** «Known but not enacted» must be representable — and it becomes a request.
11. **The floor is not KB**: logic · body constraint · the problem-solving drive. HOW he solves is KB; THAT he must is not.
12. **The body is the only extensible floor** — fixed at any moment, growable over time, and only through someone else.
