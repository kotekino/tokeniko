## THE BAR

| | |
|---|---|
| **no-abstention** | **≥ 90%** of the drill's sentences must zip with nothing left unplaced |
| **no-silent-wrong** | **100%** — a single silent-wrong fails the whole drill, at any coverage |

The asymmetry is deliberate and is parser-compiler req 8: *half-understood is legal, wrongly-understood
is the sin.* A partial zip, honestly marked, is a **pass**. A confident zip that says something the
speaker did not is a **failure even at 100% coverage**.

## THE SCORING RULES — declared with the bar, for the same reason

**PASS.** Every content element of the sentence lands in a slot, and the zip's structure is the
sentence's structure. Nothing unplaced, nothing invented.

**PARTIAL (counts as an abstention, against the 90%).** Some element could not be placed, AND the zip
says so honestly — an `OPEN` slot, the unplaced-material diagnostic, confidence lowered. The reading
is incomplete and the zip admits it.

**FAIL (silent-wrong).** The zip is complete and confident and says something the sentence does not:
a claim the speaker never made · a distinction conflated · a negation, scope or modality lost · two
different sentences producing the same zip when they mean different things.

## THE HONEST-INSTRUMENT GUARD

The drill may **force schema changes** — that is what it is for, and the Captain ruled at task 1 that
a missing slot is *«a bug, found by the instruments, fixed by redesigning the frame»*. A cluster that
decides something is the drill working, not the drill failing.

But an instrument that can always be satisfied by moving the target measures nothing. So:

1. **Every schema change the drill forces is RECORDED**, with the sentence that forced it.
2. **The COUNT of forced changes is reported beside the final score.** A drill that scores 100% after
   forcing fifteen changes says the conception was weak — and that is the honest reading, not a
   footnote.
3. **The drill is re-run whole after any change.** No per-sentence patching.
4. A defect that survives the fold is a **FAIL**, not a pending change.

## THE SIX REQUIRED CLUSTERS, and what each must DECIDE

| cluster | the question it exists to answer |
|---|---|
| **«only»** | is it a **sixth prefix element**, or does it collapse into IMPLY→EQ plus binder restriction? *(task 2's one stated doubt)* |
| **«to / toward / into / onto / as far as»** | is **`direction` a MISSING BOX**, distinct from `destination`? PropBank (`ARGM-DIR`, 1,419) and FrameNet both keep it separate *(task 5, case 7)* |
| **existential `be`** | «there is a cat» — content, not copular glue *(task 1)* |
| **`never` · `hardly` · `almost`** | structure, not description — «he almost died» asserts he did **not** *(task 2)* |
| **«twice a week»** | does `count` really hold it, or does `frequency` come back as a box? *(task 2's least confident ruling)* |
| **de re / de dicto** | does the prefix actually express both, as α claims? *(task 2, case H)* |

## THE TRAFFIC SOURCE

`tokeniko_mem.tkzipdebug` — **583 stored journeys, 245 judged mismatches.** Read-only; the biography
is not touched. *(Permission given 2026-09-14: «of course that's the right thing to do».)*

Of the 245, **114 are FORMAT failures** — the categories where v1's schema, not its senses and not
its ears, was the thing that failed:

| category | count |
|---|---|
| wrong-structure | 59 |
| dropped-content | 22 |
| missed-mood | 12 |
| operator-flattening | 10 |
| missed-negation | 8 |
| missed-modality | 2 |
| missed-quantifier | 1 |

**Excluded on purpose:** `wrong-sense` (84) is the evaluator's problem, not the format's — the parser
emits the sense slot OPEN by ruling. `ears-hallucination` (27) and `ears-translation` (2) are rag's.
Drilling them would measure the wrong layer and flatter the schema.

**Selection rule, declared now:** the ~30 are drawn to cover **every one of the seven format
categories**, weighted toward the unaddressed leads, and chosen for **structural variety** rather than
for being easy. Sentences already known to break the schema are explicitly welcome — a bar that only
admits sentences we can zip is not a bar.

## THE DELIVERABLE

- **`tk2/tkzip/schema.py`** — the shape as pydantic. It is **frame** under standing law 1 (the shape
  in which anything can be stated at all), so it belongs in code and moves only by migration.
- **`tests/fixtures/drill.py`** — every sentence hand-compiled to a literal zip, which must validate
  against the schema and round-trip through the storage form.

A schema that exists only as prose is describable, not frozen. This makes the freeze real, makes E3's
job «produce these zips from surface text», and makes every later change a migration against
something executable.
