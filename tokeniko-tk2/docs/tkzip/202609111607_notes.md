## The hypothesis, stated before testing

**Mood is not a field — it is derived from what is unbound.** Req 3 already says *one shape, three
readings: all bound = assertion · one unbound = question · unresolved = equation.* If that
generalizes, `dubitative`, `imperative`, supposition and FORECAST are all consequences of machinery
that already exists, and task 3's only genuine addition is the **binding state on a slot**.

## The four structural findings

**1. Binding state is a property of ANY slot** — a role box, a field inside a nominal record, and the
row's truth. Three values: **`BOUND` · `OPEN` · `EMPTY`**.

**2. Question and equation are the SAME zip — and this was already the Captain's ruling**, in the
2026-08-11 notes:

> *«The unbound variable IS the urge, and the choice of memory-vs-ask is only which source might bind
> it.»*

So req 3's *three* readings are really **two**: all bound = an assertion · anything open = an open
thought. Whether he **asks** it or **solves** it is a choice made later, not a shape. *(This is the
second time this session that the answer was already in the notes.)*

**3. Mood is not a field.** Question falls out of an open slot · imperative → POV(want) · supposition
→ POV(suppose) · forecast → the theatre plus confidence · irony → the figurative layer. **Nothing is
left to store.**

**4. v1's attitude `klass` enum is a category-3 set wearing a list's clothes.** *factive · doxastic ·
desiderative · reportative* — but attitude verbs are open (think, believe, suppose, want, fear,
pretend, hope, doubt…). **Ruled: geometry with a small anchor set and nearest-anchor fallback** — the
semantic catch. The heart needs «supposed or believed?» reliably, and an anchor set answers it
without a list that is silently wrong for the verb nobody thought of.

## SCALAR vs DISCRETE — a question the QM framed wrongly

The QM proposed discrete. The Captain first agreed, then **reversed his own ruling** — *«I can't
recall a reason to give up on a further level of fuzziness»* — and then made the decisive technical
point:

> *«Scalar is not harming: if we then want to collapse 0-1, scalar supports it, am I wrong?»*

**He is not wrong, and the QM's question was the wrong question.** A scalar contains the discrete
case; nothing is foreclosed; where a genuine quantity exists there is no reason to surrender the
range. **Ruled: scalar wherever there is a quantity.**

**The finding is narrower than the QM had framed it: there is no mood field to make scalar or
discrete.** A field that does not exist has no range.

**Where a scalar genuinely costs something** is on a thing that is not a quantity. `dubitative = 0.73`
has no meaning, yet the evaluator must still ROUTE the zip (assert it, or answer it) — so a
**threshold** appears, and by this project's own law a threshold is **policy**: a db row, measured,
with a note. A scalar on a non-quantity manufactures a policy question that need not have existed.
v1's `0.5 = neutral, 1.0 = question` is the tell: the midpoint does not mean «half», it means «none».

### And one thing genuinely new — where the Captain's instinct landed

> «It's cold, **isn't it**?»

The truth slot is **OPEN** (an answer is invited) but not neutral — *yes* is strongly expected.
«Is it cold?» is open with no expectation. Discrete binding cannot separate them.

**Ruled: an OPEN slot may carry a PRIOR** — a [0,1] expectation of how it will bind. Real semantic
content, scalar, and not parse confidence. This is the further level of fuzziness the Captain refused
to give up, in the place where it means something.

## WHAT TASK 4 INHERITS

**Three quantities, two slots — they must not be conflated:**

| | means | where |
|---|---|---|
| **truth** | is it so? fuzzy [0,1] | the row |
| **forecast confidence** | will it be so? | **the same slot**, when the theatre is future (heart 17) |
| **parse confidence** | did I understand the sentence? | **separate** — a property of the whole zip (parser-compiler req 4) |

A zip can be perfectly understood and probably false, or barely understood and certainly true. One
number cannot carry both.
