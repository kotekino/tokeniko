# brain — conception notes, 2026-08-13 13:12

*Socratic session, the usual shape: one-line turns, no assumptions carried in — «we don't stick to

do? Requirements distilled in `requirements.md` beside this.*

## The diagnosis: half loop, half compensator

tk1's brain is two things fused: a reasoning loop (chaining, urge collapse) and a **compensator** —
sense ladders, matching heuristics, repair work the weak dictionary forced on it. Agreed immediately:
the compensator half dies; the dual read (dictionary-review req. 19) absorbs it. What survives is
**only what geometry can never give** — choosing when answers fight, and driving the wondering.

The author's phrasing of the core, adopted: **orchestrator of inner actions, plus summarizing.**

## Summarizing is creation

Probing whether «summarize» hid more than bookkeeping: it does. **A summary mints a NEW zip** —
compression of many zips into one is creation, agreed on first pass. Which forced provenance:

- **Provenance is mandatory.** A summary carries links to the zips it compressed, and **retreats with
  them** — a summary is never allowed to outlive its parents.
- **Self-talk is a source like any other**: when he talks to himself the provenance is *himself*.

## Minting ≠ believing

The sharp question — can he disbelieve himself, or is self-provenance automatically credible? The
author, without hesitation: **he can disbelieve himself, definitely** — «how many times I create a
thought and verified it was inconsistent or plainly wrong based on my kb». So:

- every self-thought lands as a **candidate** the evaluator must pass before it enters KB;
- **wondering is self-talk in a loop**: propose, test, keep or discard.

## The service ladder — one mandatory rung

The author's architecture, arrived at through the self-talk case: **rag-typofixer / rag-translator /
parser / compiler / evaluator are static services, invoked on demand**, and the chain of calls depends
on the input:

| input | chain |
|---|---|
| Italian, sloppy | full chain |
| English | no translator |
| tkzip (external) | no parser/compiler |
| tkzip (self-talk, internal) | **only the evaluator** |

Self-talk *proves* the separation: it skips the parser entirely yet never skips evaluation. **The
parser is the sense-side translator; the evaluator is the KB's door** — two stations, only one of them
optional. The brain never climbs the ladder itself: it emits tkzip and consumes verdicts.

## WSD moves into the evaluator

One station straddled the line: sense selection. tkzip req. 10 says the sense that keeps the KB
consistent wins — and consistency needs the KB, so **WSD is the evaluator's job** (author's ruling).
The elegant consequence, agreed in one turn: **the parser emits the lemma with its sense slot open** —
ambiguity is just another unbound variable in the zip, and WSD is the evaluator solving it like any
equation. One machinery, no special case.

## The loop is dna; the how is kb

What picks the next inner action? The author's seam, via his own sleep analogy («I NEED to sleep —
physiology; but waking early or sleeping 7h is my behavior»):

- **dna**: the loop's *existence* and its timing (cpu-dependent). That he must think.
- **kb**: everything else — triggers, levels (0–1), which action fires. His personality.

### Seeding, not hardwired defaults

My proposal of a dna fallback choice for the newborn was **rejected**: «seeding is the default — a
newborn can't bear without his first seeding; to avoid hardwiring, the seeding is storing rules, and
they MAY change, as everything in kb.» Accepted. The corollary faced squarely: if every rule may
retreat, he can talk himself out of all of them — **catatonia**. Ruled a **legal state**: it happens
to biological beings too, and those beings are helped externally. The dna loop ticks on empty; rescue
comes only from outside, through the senses.

### Phases are reweighting

tk1 hardwires `swapPhase`. Under today's seam a phase is just **kb-level reweighting** (wake: senses
loud; sleep: inner actions loud, summarizing gets the cpu) — **dna only knows phases exist**; kb owns
what each one amplifies. Agreed in one turn. Later extended: **summarize and retreat are themselves
phases (or sub-phases)** the brain knows exist.

## Attention

A tick can't scan the whole KB. Agreed: **memory proposes by cosine, chaining runs on the working
set** — a small «in mind» set (recent zips, open equations, high-urge items), refreshed by geometric
retrieval.

## The determinism fence

The author asked for a pseudo-random seed «wherever we can» — nothing mechanical, nothing
predictable. The fence proposed and then sharpened by his own ruling: **truth is the invariant and it
will be deterministic** — the fuzziness already grants sufficient shades there, no randomness needed
(and this respects the logic-sacred hardwired axiom). Unpredictability lives in **choice** — collapse
tie-breaks, retrieval sampling, what to wonder next — never in whether something is believed.

## Urge physics, and everything-in-db

**Urge decay is a physiological signal** — dna physics, not influenced by experience. But the author's
operational move reaches further: even the internally-invariant data (drift parameters, all the
constants) should live **in db, not in constants files**:

> kb → db (rw) · parameters → db (r) · hardwired logic → db (r)

Then most development and deploy happens as *scripts that write the db* — deploy complexity becomes
minimal. Agreed and named: **the body shrinks to an interpreter of the db.** One tiny binary that
changes only when the *math* changes (evaluator, loop) — the irreducible core that can never be a
row. Performance rider (author): r-data is **cached in memory at boot and refreshed on a slow tick** —
which also means a parameter edit in db becomes visible **without restart**: growth through someone
else (rules #12), live.

## Retreat timing, and instinct

Does a contradiction found at input retreat on the spot, or queue for the retreat phase? **It
depends — and the threshold is kb, variable.** Which opened the author's larger idea:

**Instinct** — decisions that are *numerically consistent and iterated* (all the single actions that
evaluate some threshold) may be wrapped in **micro-neural-nets** learned with positive feedback loops.
Micro-deciders: experience-based, less structured than a kb rule, blackbox-ish. Two fences, both
agreed 100%:

1. **Instinct only ever picks among options already legal** — a blackbox has no provenance, so it may
   never mint or admit a belief; truth stays deterministic.
2. **A kb rule outranks instinct, always** — the author's own phrasing: «my instinct says x, but I
   know it's WRONG, so I don't do x.»

## Reward — and the Heart

Instinct's feedback loop needs a scalar. **An open equation closing is one reward** (the
problem-solving drive made numeric) — but the author: «we need to think of others.» And named the
missing organ: **the heart** — foreseen in tk1, never built, absolutely necessary. An emotional reward
is *equally* important as an intellectual one: «I solved it!» beside «I made her happy and that fills
me with joy.»

The mechanism, phrased and confirmed («it is exactly this»): **the heart is a second reward family
whose scalar is moved by evaluated states of others** — he reads her joy through the senses, and a kb
value («her joy moves me», weight, sign) lets it move his own levels. **Empathy as machinery,
attachment as kb.** Same physics as urges, different source — the world's states instead of his own
equations. And the mirror comes free: signed weights include **grief**, legal like catatonia.

The heart is kept as its **own chapter** in the requirements (author's close): part of the brain's
conception, distinct enough to grow separately.
