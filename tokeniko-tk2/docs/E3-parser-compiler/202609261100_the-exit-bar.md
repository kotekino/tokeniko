## The argument

**Req 8 makes a finite exit possible where «100%» is not.** *Half-understood is legal,
wrongly-understood is the sin.* The station cannot reach «understands everything», but it can reach
«never lies»: every truth error can be closed either by a fix or by an HONEST ABSTENTION — withhold
the clause, leave the word unplaced — and abstaining is always available and cheap. So the exit
demands honesty in full and coverage only as a ratchet.

**Three kinds of failure, three bars:**

| kind | what it is | bar |
|---|---|---|
| **a wrong claim** | a complete zip that says what was not said; the evaluator would store a false belief | **0 known** |
| **a silent loss** | the zip claims less and says nothing of it — req 8's «silently complete» | **0 known** |
| **quality** | partial and honestly marked · the decompiler phrases it otherwise · the station abstains | **a ratchet** |

**And «0 known» is only as good as the search.** «She lives here» and «John exclaimed to Marie»
were outside every corpus; the drill's 87 and the UD gate's 43 are ours, or tuned against. So the
bar needs one FRESH set, declared before it is run — `db/0038`'s held-out method at the scale of the
epic.

**Costs, stated both ways.** This bar: the fresh set will surface new wrong claims or silent losses and cost one more loop;
abstaining lowers coverage on real text; ~100 zips judged by hand. Without it: E4 is built on a
station whose false beliefs were never counted, and in a logic-first mind a false input is a false
theorem, undone by retreat and not by an edit. A looser bar (wrong claims only, corpora only) would have passed
both truth errors found this morning.

## `E3.12.5` — the six rulings the sort needed *(the Captain, 2026-09-26 — «agreed on all six»)*

1. **What remains must be ENTAILED.** A partial zip is quality only if what it still claims is
   entailed by the sentence; otherwise it is a wrong claim, and the station WITHHOLDS the clause.
   Cutting a word out from under a negation or an attitude widens the claim («you need not drive» →
   «you need nothing») even when the word is recorded. Settles `E3.3.11.6`, `E3.12.1.3`, `E3.12.1.4`,
   `E3.3.2.7.1`.
2. **An empty theatre means UNSTATED, never «now».** «I could swim» losing its past is a silent loss,
   closed by recording it (`E3.3.2.10.5`).
3. **An `Open` described only by number is SAID BACK, never asked** — only an interrogative (`sort`)
   asks, which is what schema v4 meant. «They love one another» stops being a question (`E3.3.13`).
4. **A decompiler that says the REVERSE is a wrong claim**, not quality — it would tell a person a
   falsehood: «the day she bore» (`E3.3.2.5.1.2`), the dropped «not» (`E3.12.1.5`).
5. **`aw-13` is WITHHELD** — «All that glitters is not gold» is ambiguous between ¬∀ and ∀¬, and the
   «may not» precedent withholds an ambiguity; the drill is amended to follow (`E3.12.1.2`).
6. **«I do not think that he sleeps» compiles LITERALLY** — ¬think(p). It is a wrong claim under either
   reading, so this decides only the target zip; which verbs neg-raise is per-verb knowledge, later
   (`E3.3.11.2`).
