## One station, one format

The author's opening question — tk1 goes through a 2-step format (AST → LLC + zip); shall tk2 use one
format only and concentrate parsing and compiling in one core lib? Agreed at once: **tkzip is the
only thing that ever crosses the boundary** (stored, queued, compared — sense slots open, per
evaluator #5). Inside the lib a working scratch will always exist (dependency tree, coreference
bookkeeping) — **private scratch, never a contract** (the author's words): not stored, not exposed,
no second format to keep in sync. tk1's AST/LLC pair dies as an interface and survives as ephemeral
working state.

## Stanza — kept, demoted, swappable

Is stanza still the right base? Yes, with a demotion: tk2 has stripped the parser of everything
stanza was *bad* at — senses to the evaluator, semantics to the anchors, figures to the derived
layer. What remains is **skeleton extraction** (tokens, POS, dependency edges), where stanza is
best-in-class, local, laptop-honest. But it is a **proposer behind the lib boundary**: the station's
contract is surface → tkzip, the skeleton provider is an implementation detail — swappable without
touching any format. A stumbling skeleton is rag-in's escalation case.

## Protecting against the trained model's small error rate

Three shields were laid out; the author sharpened their geography — shields 2 and 3 belong to the
*journey*, not the parser itself: **(1)** disagreement between skeletons (noted: spaCy and stanza are
not consistently comparable in tk1 — their POS/dep vocabularies differ, so this shield is weak);
**(2)** the evaluator as second reader — a confidently-wrong parse grounds incoherently, and
incoherence on fresh input flags *parse-suspicion*, not speaker error (mandatory step); **(3)** below
the floor, **the brain asks** — the mouth renders the reading back («so you mean…?») and
misunderstanding becomes dialogue. Last resort stands: `original` is kept verbatim; even a landed
misreading is retreatable.

## The confidence scalar — tk1 never had one

The author's real question: a confidence level on the zipping itself — «0.84 confidence that what I
zipped IS what I received». The design, agreed:

- **confidence = a calibrated aggregate of signals the station already produces**: **coverage**
  (fraction of tokens that landed in the zip) · **repairs taken** (every tolerance fallback pays a
  known cost) · **self-round-trip** (zip → scaffold → re-zip: does it re-read as itself? no rag
  involved);
- the aggregation weights are the **seventh micro-nn site** — legal (steers choice, not truth),
  rewarded by the journey: a speaker's correction trains it down, smooth dialogue up, so 0.84 comes
  to MEAN 0.84;
- **the scalar rides on the zip forever** — memory keeps how well he understood *then*;
- thresholds drive the journey: high → proceed, mid → rag-in, low → the brain asks.

**The performance amendment** (author's fear: enormously slow): the ladder pays the rag law — the
cheap signals are free bookkeeping inside the one pass; **the self-round-trip is escalation-only**,
fired when the cheap band is ambiguous, plus lazily in sleep to spot-calibrate. Clean input costs ONE
parse, exactly as today; only suspicion pays the second read.

## Purity, honesty, symmetry, and the gate

- **The station is pure — context is an argument, never state.** Anaphora, ellipsis, fragments
  resolve against the channel's recent zips *handed in by the caller*; the same lib serves every
  caller with zero hidden memory.
- **Half-understood is legal, wrongly-understood is the sin.** Where the sentence exceeds the format,
  the station emits a partial zip honestly marked — caught parts bound, the rest open variables
  (tkzip #2), confidence saying so — never a silently-complete nearest fit.
- **The station is bidirectional** — the renderer (zip → faithful scaffold) lives in the same lib;
  parse and render stay symmetric, the round-trip is internal machinery.
- **The fifty-sentence drill is the acceptance gate** (inherited from README §6) — the bar set in
  advance (§8's coverage test): the proportion of ordinary sentences that must zip *without*
  abstention, drilled before any officer builds.
