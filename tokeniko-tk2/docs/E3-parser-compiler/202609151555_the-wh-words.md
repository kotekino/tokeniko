*Roadmap: `E3.2.6`*

*«There is no mood field» — a question is something OPEN. The rows said that much and stopped

wh-words **five ways**, and three of the five do not open a box at all.*

## THE FIVE KINDS *(closed classes v5, `db/0011`)*

    box          when · where · how · whither · whence   ->  time, location, manner, destination, source
    participant  who · whom · what · which               ->  THE DEPENDENCY decides
    truth        whether                                 ->  the row's own truth slot
    antecedent   why                                     ->  an OPEN row implying this one
    field        whose                                   ->  the possessor, inside the record

**`who` AND `what` NAME NO ROLE, and that is requirement 12 again.** A participant's role arrives by
RELATION — `nsubj` → agent, `obj` → patient — so the row says «a participant» and the compiler reads
the dependency it already reads for every other nominal. A role written into the row would be a
third place the same fact lives.

**`whether` OPENS THE TRUTH SLOT**, which is the polar question E2 already ruled: *«Is the cat
hungry?» has every box bound and its truth open.* The word is the subordinate spelling of it.

## «WHY» IS THE ONE WORTH THE PAGE

**There is no cause box to open, because there is no CAUSE relation** (req 37): *a cause is `IMPLY`
read with the theatre's arrow of time.* So «why do you sleep?» is not a question about a slot. It
asks for an unknown **ANTECEDENT**:

    r0       you sleep                  CLAIMED
    r0_why   (predicate OPEN, no boxes)  —
    j0       IMPLY (r0_why, r0)         CLAIMED

**The collapse pays for itself here, and the shape was already in the format.** Had `CAUSE` survived
as a relation, «why» would have wanted a cause box; that box would have needed a filler that is a
whole proposition; and tkzip would have grown a second way of saying what `IMPLY` already says. The
sleeping stays asserted — only its antecedent is asked.

## TWO BUGS THE QUESTIONS EXPOSED

**1. `who` READ AS A RELATIVE IN «WHO SLEEPS?»** The table holds both readings and lists `relative`
first under `nsubj`; nothing in the dependency separates them, because «WHO sleeps» and «the cat WHO
sleeps» are both `nsubj` of their own clause. **tk1's R5 walk is what decides**: a wh-word in the
ROOT clause asks, one inside a relative clause describes. `ClosedClasses.read` gained
`in_root_clause`, and `walk_skeleton` supplies it because it is the caller that holds the tree.

*R5 was earned by tk1 on live specimens in July and written into this station's skeleton for the
wh-position test. This is its second job, and it was already there.*

**2. `be` AS ROOT BECAME `be.n`.** «Where is the cat?» has `be` as its root because there is no other
verb — and `AUX` had no entry in the POS map, so the key defaulted to a NOUN. Two fixes: `AUX` is
verbal, and **a copular root earns no predicate at all** (req 31). The row is a LOCATION question
about the cat, with no predicate and `be` gone entirely.

**Existential `be` is the exception req 31 names** — «there is a cat» IS content — and the expletive
is what separates them. The row for `be` already states the conditional («structure when `cop`»);
this is the same judgement one dependency further out, where the word is the root and there is no
`cop` to read.

**The first two are the thirteen ambiguous markers** — the head-verb geometry, which needs the
