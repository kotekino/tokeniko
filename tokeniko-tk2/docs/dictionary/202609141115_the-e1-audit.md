## ❌ REQ 2 — «eat stays near food» is REFUTED AT SCALE

> *«No precision gain may cost an existing correct nearness; the nearness lives in D (+0.405) and
> survives.»*

| | PoC (975 dims) | built base (4,555 dims) |
|---|---|---|
| `eat.v ~ food.n`, **D cosine** | +0.405 | **+0.0484** |
| `eat.v ~ food.n`, **D direct cell** | — | **0.0000** |
| blended, as the reader returns it | — | **+0.0180 → ABSTAIN** |

It is **bar pair #0**, declared NEAR, carrying the note *«the Captain's line: eat and food are
geometrically similar, do not lose it»*.

### It is not a threshold problem — the ordering is wrong

> `bed.n ~ cause.n`, declared **FAR** → **+0.0470**
> `eat.v ~ food.n`, declared **NEAR** → **+0.0180**

**The FAR pair scores 2.6× higher than the NEAR pair.** No floor separates those. Across the whole
bar, **11 of the 16 declared-NEAR pairs score below the highest declared-FAR pair** (`compass` at
+0.2721): the two classes overlap heavily and the geometry separates only the easy top.

### Why «0 wrong» hid it

Both land in `ABSTAIN`, and ABSTAIN counts as *undecided*, never *wrong*. The convention is honest and
it made a requirement failure invisible. **That is the lesson, not the number.**

### THE CAUSE, measured — and it makes the fix straightforward

The D cell is **exactly 0.0000**: `eat.v` and `food.n` share no gloss words at all. D measures *«what
two definitions SHARE»*. The actual relation is *«eat's definition NAMES food»*:

```
eat.v.01    take in solid FOOD
bed.n.01    a piece of furniture that provides a place to SLEEP
hungry.a.01 feeling a need or desire to EAT food
```

**Both edges the Captain curated by hand are instances of one mechanizable rule — A's gloss names B —
and `eat~food` is the same shape.** The signal is already computed: `glosses.lexicon_words_in` walks
A's gloss for base words for the closure, and then it is thrown away for R's purposes.

It belongs in **R**, not D: it is STATED in the resource, DIRECTIONAL (`eat` names `food`; `food` does
not name `eat`), accountable per cell, and its evidence is a quotable string. That is req 18's
contract exactly, and req 20 already ruled the shape of such an edge.

*Two caveats, both measurable rather than guessable:* it would roughly **double R** (~5 base words per
gloss × 4,555 keys), and it carries noise — `bed.n`'s gloss also names *furniture, place, ground,
plants*. Whether that breaks a declared-FAR pair is T3's measurement. And it will **not** fix
`drink~water`: drink's gloss says «liquids».

## ❌ REQ 10 — «never blended into one float» is CONTRADICTED BY THE READER

The requirement, as written:

> *«two matrices over one key space — R (named edges, signed, sparse, accountable) and D (gloss
> co-occurrence, unsigned, dense, topical) — **consulted separately, every answer naming its source,
> never blended into one float**.»*

`distribution.py`'s own docstring repeats it: *«a query consults the one that can honestly answer it.
**Neither is folded into the other, here or anywhere downstream.**»*

**The reader returns `cos(R + 0.15·D)` — one float.** The separate reads exist (`relations_of`) but
are not what `similarity()` and `verdict()` use. The contradiction is visible *inside the codebase*:
the matrix layer asserts no blending, the query layer blends.

**The Captain's ruling, 2026-09-14: separate.** *«That was a mistake. We chose two arrays because the
use case of inspecting one or another was clear. Blending is for sure something that can have
benefit, but it is against what we thought and we did for a reason.»*

**req 15 — membership. THE QM'S FIRST READING WAS WRONG AND IS CORRECTED HERE.** `chew`, `love`,

chew` cannot be a base-to-base R cell, because `chew` is not an axis.* `want`, `swallow`, `negation`
and `runway` **did** become dimensions — the repair is real and partial.

**req 16 — `land.n~land.v`** is 0.410 → **+0.1695**. The `derivational` down-weight to 0.45 helped;
declared FAR, reads ABSTAIN. Improved, not closed. Correctly still open.
