## WHAT ARE A PARSED BELIEF'S PARENTS?

`Provenance` (E0, `tk2/core/mixins.py`) requires `parents` to be **non-empty by design**, and the
model says why: *«mixing this in is a claim that the row is derived, and a derived row with no parents
is the claim contradicting itself.»* Requirement 5 here states the same: *every derived thing names
its parents; summaries retreat with them.*

For two of the three producers this is already answered:

| the belief | its parents |
|---|---|
| a **summary** (brain req 3 — summarizing is creation) | the memories it compressed |
| a **chained** conclusion | its premises |
| **a sentence somebody said** | **? — the open question** |

A parsed belief's honest parent is **the utterance it came from**. That has consequences this chapter
owns and E2 does not:

- either the **incoming utterance is itself a row** (so it has an id to point at) — which makes every
  heard sentence a stored artefact, and interacts with the biography rules and with `original`;
- or **parsed beliefs carry provenance differently** from derived ones — which weakens the invariant
  that every belief can name where it came from;
- or the **channel/message row** already planned for the senses tier is the parent, and this is
  simply a matter of naming it.

**Why it matters beyond tidiness:** provenance is the retreat mechanism (brain req 4). A belief whose
provenance is «somebody said it» and who cannot say *who, where, when* cannot be retreated when the
source turns out to be untrustworthy — and trust is per-source (the trust ledger, learning-from-others).

**Related:** `derived_by` should carry the STATION VERSION for parsed beliefs (`station-v2.3`), which
is exactly the use E0 documents for that field — *«a rebuild replaces one producer's output wholesale:
this is the key that makes a whole derived tier revocable in one sweep»*. If a station version is
later found buggy, that is how its zips are found and re-parsed.
