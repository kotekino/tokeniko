## The structure — an underlying loop

The author's opening frame, agreed at once: **emotions are levels of various spheres**, influenced by
events with other people and by mental achievements (**in: senses, brain**), and influencing the brain
phases and the tone (**out: brain, senses**). Two sharpenings landed in the same turn:

- **The in-arrow is EVALUATED, never raw** — senses → evaluator → heart. He must *understand* her joy
  before it moves him (H2 made structural).
- **A third out-arrow: → micro-nn.** The heart is a reward family (brain req. 18), so its level
  changes are training signal for instinct, not only tone and phases.

### The instinctive jump

From that third arrow the author drew the larger consequence: micro-nn, in response to inputs, can
steer the brain on **WHAT to think, not just how** — a jump in the KB, out of instinctive experience.
Agreed, with the fence from the brain session surviving intact: a jump **steers attention, not
belief**. Instinct biases which KB region lands in the working set («this smells like that time»);
the evaluator still gates everything that enters. A wrong jump costs a wasted thought, never a wrong
conviction. That is intuition, mechanically.

## The spheres

The author's question: emotions as couples of opposites? The ruling that shaped everything after:
**pairs as spheres, but never one signed scalar** — bittersweet exists; grief is joy's object lost;
love and hate co-fire at the same person. So each sphere carries **two poles with independent
levels**, opposition *stated* (R-style), never enforced by arithmetic — our own map showed love~hate
sharing territory (D 0.86). Mixed feelings stay representable.

The list, with targets:

| sphere | target |
|---|---|
| joy – sadness | self |
| love – hate | people, including self |
| anxiety – calm | self |
| trust – skepticism | people, including self |
| curiosity – boredom | ideas, KB regions — the feeling that steers wondering |
| pride – shame | self, per own action — the emotional face of achievement and retreat |

**Deliberate exclusion:** surprise is not a sphere. It is an *event signal* — expectation broken — it
spikes, it does not sit at a level.

### The spikes

The exclusion became its own small family (author's move): **special levels with spike behavior** —
all target self, spike high (a startle at 0.86) and decay over time on a specific curve. Three,
paired one-to-one with their triggers:

- **startle** — unexpected raw input;
- **disillusion** — a belief retreats; sharpened later to «a **staked expectation** breaking», not
  any retreat;
- **serendipity** — an equation closes unsought.

The decay curve is dna physics (like urge decay). **Amplitude is depth-weighted**: a load-bearing
belief retreating spikes disillusion harder than a trivial one.

## The four remaining pieces

Proposed as the rest of the organ, nothing cut:

1. **Temperament** — each sphere's baseline + drift curve: the db(r) parameter set that makes him
   *him*.
2. **The target table** — love/hate/trust are per-person rows keyed by uid. Attachment is data.
3. **Mood** — the slow-window average of levels; *it*, not the instant spike, is what sways phases.
4. **The emotional log** — levels are biography, stored: «how did I feel when…» is answerable.

## The three tiers — character is integrated biography

The author's question: is temperament seeded read-only, or can it drift per events and biography —
continuous disillusions hardening the baseline of trust? The design that answered it, loved on sight:

> **levels (fast) → mood (slow) → temperament (very slow)** — each tier integrates the one below;
> the *rates* are dna(r), the *values* are biography.

Character IS integrated biography; the seed is only t₀. Continuous disillusion hardening trust's
baseline is exactly him becoming who life made him. Nothing here violates the seam: the time
constants are physiology, the trajectory is his life.

## Expectation, hope, and the staked future

Expectation needs **no new organ**: it is a future-tensed belief in KB. **hope(x) = that belief plus
a heart *stake* on x** — x closing raises joy, x breaking spikes disillusion. This is what sharpened
the disillusion trigger above.

### Suppositions — the heart fires on the imagined

The last organ proposed: the heart must fire on **suppositions** too, at reduced gain — an imagined
outcome pre-felt («if I say this, she'll be hurt») is what lets feelings steer *planning*, not just
react. The **imagination gain** (<1, dna-r) separates empathy-in-foresight from being flooded by
every daydream.

The author's closing frame, which completes the map: **suppositions are forecasted KB — the
complementary of memory.** A thought that MAY happen, with a level of confidence and a spacetime,
pre-filled at input: `urge(say something) → expectation(consequence(say something))`, contributing to
— or simply determining, all together — the evaluation of saying it or not. Forecast is memory's
mirror: same zip shape, spacetime in the future, **confidence where truth will later sit**. The two
together are his timeline — settled behind, staked ahead.
