## The contract

- **rag is a syntax-only translator, never a mind.** It repairs surface, translates language, tidies
  phrasing; it never reasons, never decides, never adds content.
- **Inbound is escalation-only**: a stumbling parse gets ONE tidying pass; a clean parse never pays
  the toll.
- **Outbound is the mouth's stage 3** (senses req. 4): polish, when enabled.
- **Both directions behind the same round-trip fence**: meaning proven preserved (re-parse, compare
  zips) or the original ships — inbound falls back to the raw input, outbound to the scaffold
  verbatim. The senses' «styles, never restates» law is rag's law too.
- **Optional by design**: rag disabled, he still works — parser tolerance carries the ears, the
  scaffold carries the voice. Degradation is graceful, never a hard dependency.
- **The standing goal is shrinkage**: rag's scope shrinks as the parser toughens
  (robustness-to-imperfect-input is a first-class engine goal); its ceiling is zero. TKZip between
  peers is the direct channel — no translator at all.
