## CASE 1 — the answer came from task 3, not from a new field

The QM's instinct was **per-row** confidence, so the brain could ask a targeted question («so you mean
the *dog*?»). That is wrong, and parser-compiler req 8 already said why:

> *«caught parts bound, the rest open variables, confidence saying so»*

If the station genuinely could not tell *dog* from *cat*, the honest encoding is **`noun` = OPEN with
a prior** — not BOUND to a guess carrying a low number. **The per-part uncertainty is already carried
by the binding state and the prior ruled at task 3.**

**And parse confidence can only be one number, for a reason that is not aesthetic:** its calibration
signal is *«did the speaker correct me?»*, which arrives **per utterance**, not per row. A per-row
scalar would have no training signal and could never come to *mean* anything — defeating the notes'
own goal that *«0.84 comes to MEAN 0.84»*.

## CASE 2 — confidence is a SLOT, and may be EMPTY

Self-talk invokes no parser (the service ladder: *tkzip internal → only the evaluator*). So parse
confidence is **not 1.0** — that would claim perfect understanding of an utterance that never
happened. It is `EMPTY`, which is exactly the binding state ruled at task 3. **The same machinery,
a third time.**

`original` is likewise absent for self-talk and for chained zips, so it cannot be a required zip
field; and its only consumer is *retreat of a landed misreading*, which happens on stored beliefs.
**Document-level.**

*(No tension with the confidence ruling: the two have different lifetimes. Confidence is read by the
journey's thresholds BEFORE storage, so it travels with the zip in flight. `original` is read only at
retreat, long after.)*

## CASE 4 — two things that look alike and are not

- **POV(Anna·say)** is **content**: the sentence is *about* Anna saying something.
- **Provenance** is **where this belief reached me**: if Bob told me «Anna said the cat is hungry»,
  provenance is Bob and the POV is Anna·say.

Both present, neither reducible to the other. The test confirms H2 rather than breaking it.

## CASE 7 — the stored zip never mutates

Heart 17 says confidence sits where truth will later sit. It does **not** mean the slot changes over
time. When tomorrow comes:

- a **new** memory is minted — «it rained», past theatre, truth bound;
- the forecast's `status` moves STAKED → resolved and `resolved_at` is set (E0 built both);
- the heart spikes — serendipity or disillusion (heart 8);
- **the forecast's zip is untouched.** *«I expected rain at 0.7»* stays true forever: that is
  biography.

**Retreat, not override**, applied to the future half of the timeline.

**One requirement this exposes for E4:** evaluator req 12 matches arriving events against staked
forecasts, so **the matcher must compare content while IGNORING the theatre's tense** — «it will
rain» and «it rained» must match on content and differ only in time.

## TWO FINDINGS HANDED TO OTHER CHAPTERS

Neither is a tkzip question, so neither was decided inside E2. Written into their chapters on the
Captain's ruling of 2026-09-12 («now», rather than parked):

1. **`docs/E0-data-modeling/` — what are a PARSED belief's parents?** `Provenance` requires `parents`
   non-empty by design. A summary's are memories, a chained zip's are premises — but a sentence
   someone said has an **utterance** as its parent, which must therefore be a row, or parsed beliefs
   need different handling.
2. **`docs/E0-datatier/` — the retreat cascade has no reverse index.** Child → parent is a direct id
   lookup and works. The cascade needs **parent → children** («what was built on the belief that just
   fell») and `parents.id` is indexed nowhere — today a full scan of every KB collection.
