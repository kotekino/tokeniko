11. **The station consults the dictionary for SHAPE, never for MEANING** *(added 2026-09-15)* — the ported content lists become geometry with a nearest-anchor fallback, so «is this an implication verb?» is answered by the base rather than by a list; but the sense slot is emitted **OPEN** and the station never picks a sense. So the instrument is a fact the resource STATES, not a distance it implies — see req 15. Record: `202609160929_the-ambiguous-markers.md`.]**

12. **The 37 → 18 mapping has TWO HALVES, and they must not collide** *(found by the gate, 2026-09-15)* — a role that arrives with a MARKER (`location`, `time`, `instrument`, …) is the closed classes' business and lives in rows; a role that arrives by RELATION (`agent`, `patient`, `recipient`) is the compile core's and comes from `nsubj`/`obj`/`iobj`. The table proves the split by holding **no marker for `patient` or `experiencer`** — there is none in English. Asking the marker table for a relational role is a category error, and it was made twice in the gate's own first transcription.

13. **A joining word says whether its clauses are CLAIMED** *(closed classes v4, `db/0010`, 2026-09-15)* — «if» and «because» are the same `IMPLY` and differ only in assertion status (req 38), and nothing in the dependency tree separates them: both are `mark` on an `advcl`. So `asserts` is knowledge about the word and lives in the row — `both` · `neither` · `matrix` (a POV: the report is claimed, its content is not) · `ambiguous` («when», where only the theatre decides, and the station abstains). **The root is not claimed by being the root**: «if it rains I stay home» asserts neither half. **[AMENDED 2026-09-18 — `or` and `nor` assert NEITHER (`db/0017`, the Captain): a disjunction claims the disjunction, not its halves, and `nor` claiming both halves contradicted its own join. Free choice under a modal («can be X or can be Y» meaning both are possible) is the exception, and it is not built.]**

14. **A wh-word says WHICH slot it opens, and three of the five kinds are not boxes** *(closed classes v5, `db/0011`, 2026-09-15)* — `box` (when → time, where → location, how → manner) · `participant` (who/what: **the relation decides**, req 12) · `truth` (whether: the polar question) · **`antecedent` (why: there is no cause box to open — req 37 — so it asks for an unknown ROW implying the one asserted)** · `field` (whose: the possessor lives inside the record, req 26). **The same word asks in a root clause and describes in a relative one**, and only tk1's R5 walk separates them: nothing in the dependency does. **[AMENDED 2026-09-16 — THERE ARE THREE READINGS AND THIS LINE NAMES TWO.** «I know WHO did it» is neither: an embedded question opens its slot exactly as a root one does, and only the utterance's MOOD differs. R5 answers the mood question rightly and was then read as «therefore relative», which left `who` unplaced. **UD does mark this one**, contrary to the line above: a relative clause modifies a NOUN (`acl:relcl`) while an embedded question is a clausal COMPLEMENT — so R5 decides the mood and the clause's own dependency decides between describing and asking. A wh-word with no free-relative row falls back to the INTERROGATIVE reading, not to whatever is first: `why` has none, and an embedded «why» still asks.]**

15. **What a marker means is settled by the SUPERSENSE of the words around it, and the rules are curation** *(closed classes v6, `db/0012`, 2026-09-16)* — «I swam in the pool» and «I left in May» differ in nothing a dependency tree can see, and everything in what kind of thing the nominal IS. WordNet files every synset under one of **26 noun and 15 verb lexicographer classes**, published and closed, so the table from a supersense to a tkzip role can be **complete** — the same argument that made UD the station's gate (req 10), arriving a second time at the other end of the mapping. The PRIMARY sense only (`db/0006`'s rule; a max over senses reads `pool` as a TIME). **`of` asks no dictionary at all** — the head's POS settles material from possessor, and the possessor is a FIELD (req 26), not a box. **A DEFAULT IS AN ANSWER AND IT IS COUNTED**: req 8 forbids the *silently* complete nearest fit, so an unchosen marker fills its box from the curation's best-first order and the zip records that nothing chose (`Compiled.defaulted`, req 4's input). **Named debt**: the supersense is read live from WordNet and sits outside the base's build fingerprint; its home is a `lexname` on `SenseVectorDoc`.

16. **A NAME IS ITS OWN POINT, AND IT IS FIVE QUESTIONS AND NOT ONE** *(the Captain, 2026-09-16, reopening the parked hole — E3b)* — *is this a name at all* (casing is an indicator and not a test: tk1's gate both admitted OOV gibberish spaCy labelled `GPE` and refused «kotekino», which is lower case and known) · *a name of WHAT* (person · public figure · organization · place · work · event) · *WHICH one* (Paris, France against Paris, Ontario — tk1 logged this and never solved it) · *have I MET it* (recognition against a names table, which is a different question from identification and is the one that carries identity) · *what it IS in a zip* (a type-centroid SEMANTIC vector plus a context-scoped IDENTITY uid, never merged). **The geographical half is CURATED and inherited** — tk1's places table, ~4.7M author-curated rows with two containment chains and a closed ~21-value `type` column that is an `is_a` statement, read lazily and never materialized. **The prominence half cannot be curated and is the ecosystem's first micro-nn** (micro-nn req 9). **An NER label is a CANDIDATE, never a verdict** — which is exactly what the micro-nn fence is allowed to rank. *This does NOT reopen req 2: entity recognition is not a UD task, has no UD relation, and stanza ships its own NER processor — so «stanza against spaCy on the same text» is a measurement E3b owes rather than a second opinion on a dependency parse.* Record: `202609160959_the-name-question.md`.

17. **THE KINDS OF NAME ARE OUR OWN INVENTORY, AND THEY ARE ROWS — NOT CODE, AND NEVER A THIRD PARTY'S** *(the Captain, 2026-09-16, answering open question 14)* — *«with our inventory as db (not code!), we can modify it keeping the logic: everything is KB. If we rely on a third party we lose this ability.»* **That is the first standing law's FRAME test, applied to a set that feels like law and is not**: «public figure» against «person» is a judgement about the world, revisable, and a roster needing a code change to grow would put the table back in code — the precedent is `ClosedClassDoc.word_class`, a string and not an enum for this exact reason. So BOTH halves are curation: the kinds themselves, and the map from an NER label to a kind. Only the reader is frame. **An NER label set could not be the frame anyway**, for four further reasons: seven of OntoNotes' eighteen (`DATE TIME PERCENT MONEY QUANTITY ORDINAL CARDINAL`) are not names but measurements the format already says, so adopting them gives tkzip a second way of saying what it has (the defect req 37's collapse was avoided for) · three (`GPE LOC FAC`) are one question for us, «is it in the places table» · **a kind must be reachable with NO label at all** («kotekino» gets `PROPN` and no NER type — tk1's recorded failure, repaired only by bolting on a second path) · and it is the shape the station already uses twice, at req 12 and req 15. **Prominence is a SCALAR on the resolution, not a kind**: «public personality» is a person who is prominent, and a company is an `organization` that is prominent. Made a kind, it would have to be decided before the thing is identified and would leave the micro-nn nothing to rank; made a number, it IS what the micro-nn ranks (req 16, E3b task 5). Record: `202609160959_the-name-question.md`.

19. **AN ADVERB'S SCOPE IS CURATION, AND CURATABLE ONLY BECAUSE MANNER IS THE DEFAULT** *(`db/0013`, 2026-09-16 — the four-way split of tkzip req 23, made operable)* — **the resource cannot answer it**: WordNet files every adverb under ONE lexicographer class (`adv.all`) and adverbs have **no hypernyms at all**, so both the supersense of req 15 and R's taxonomy are silent. What makes an OPEN class enumerable is that **79% of English's 3,767 single-word adverbs are `-ly` and describe the action**, so the rows hold the 106 EXCEPTIONS — `epistemic` → the prefix's modality (the adverbial spelling of `may`/`must`) · `evaluative` → an attitude whose holder is the speaker, and therefore an ABSTENTION until the POV can carry it · `discourse` → a JOIN with the row before it, abstaining when there is no row before it (the other half is context, an argument by req 7) · `circumstantial` → a box, and `roles` says which. **A default is COUNTED, never silent** (req 8's distinction, as req 15 drew it for the markers). **THEY LIVE IN THEIR OWN COLLECTION** because the closed-class forms filter D's vocabulary and sixty new ones would desynchronise the sealed base — the cost of two rosters is paid by making only the ROSTER second: `compiled` holds `ClosedClassDoc.compiled`'s own vocabulary, so a reader cannot tell which table answered, and the rows merge upward the day the exclusion set stops being load-bearing. **A MARKED NOMINAL OUTRANKS A BARE ADVERB** for the same box: «left EARLY in the MORNING» has two time expressions and one box, and the speaker chose the marker. Record: `202609161251_the-adverbs.md`.

20. **⚑ THE PERSON AXIS ROTATES UNDER A POINT OF VIEW, AND NOTHING IN tk2 KNOWS IT** *(the Captain, 2026-09-16 — asked, answered NO, and recorded)* — the blueprint's only sentence on the subject is the second standing law's *«a pronoun is indexical, so it is resolved to an ENTITY at parse time»*, which says what to do and never says that **the context it resolves against CHANGES inside a POV**. The rule: a first-person pronoun names the HOLDER of the innermost POV it is under, a second-person one names that POV's ADDRESSEE, and with no POV the outer speech act supplies both — «John said to Marie: YOU are a clever girl» means Marie, and «John thinks I am wrong» still means the speaker, because a thinking POV addresses nobody. **Four separate gaps**: there is no person axis at all (`compile()` takes no context argument — req 7 is written and not built); the axis must ROTATE, and **the addressee is a slot tkzip does not have** (`Pov` holds holder · verb · strength), so where it lives is a FORMAT ruling under a frozen schema (req 73); **stanza splits a quote into a separate sentence** and the station has no multi-sentence path, so the rotation information and the pronouns it governs arrive in different skeletons; and **neither gate tests it** — the drill has no quoted speech at all, and this QM transcribed `ccomp` as the INDIRECT «he said that…» while passing over UD's own direct-quote examples on the same page. **A wrong rotation is a wrong BELIEF, not a wrong parse**: the zip is well-formed, internally coherent, and about the wrong person, so the evaluator cannot catch it. **[BUILT 2026-09-16, the same day it was asked. The Captain ruled the FORMAT gains the field — `SCHEMA_VERSION = 3`, `addressee: Box | None` on `Pov` and `AttitudeRow`, the first migration of the frozen schema (tkzip req 73) — rather than let the resolver read a `say` row's recipient, which would have put a closed set of verbs in code. EMPTY is the point and not `Open()`: «no addressee» and «addressed to somebody unknown» are different facts, and only the first stops a rotation, which is what keeps «John thinks I am wrong» meaning the speaker. The rotation is decided BEFORE the clauses are compiled, because a pronoun resolves where it is met and its attitude is built later. And across a sentence boundary a FRAME — a saying with an agent and a recipient — rotates the sentence after it, which is what makes «John said to Marie "You are a clever girl"» mean Marie. STILL OWED: the quote becoming the CONTENT of the saying, and cases in both gates. Record: `202609161544_the-rotation.md`.]** Record: `202609161349_the-person-axis.md`.

21. **A QUESTION BELONGS TO A STATEMENT, AND THE `?` DECIDES WHICH** *(the Captain, 2026-09-18 — E3 task 2c)* — «Is the cat hungry?» compiled at truth 1.0, a question stored as a belief. **The `?` token decides**: with it the statement asks, without it nothing does, and `!` is not `?`. Word order is not read — it cannot see «The cat is hungry?», whose syntax stanza reads CORRECTLY as declarative, because English writes that question only in the punctuation. **Main, coordinate and quoted clauses are statements**; a subordinate clause belongs to its statement and asks only through its own word (`whether`, req 14). Stanza attaches the `?` to the root, so the station gives it to **the last statement it closes**; one that already asks through a wh-word keeps its truth. A missing `?` is the pre-input layer's to restore, not the station's to guess. **Frame**: the `?` is the decoded tree's punctuation, and decoding is frame (root `CLAUDE.md`, 2026-09-18). **[AND THE SUBORDINATE SPELLING, 2026-09-18 — req 14's «`whether` opens the truth» had never fired: `mark` always read the subordinator row. The rows hold both readings (`db/0016` gives `if` the one `whether` had) and the CLAUSE picks — a complement (`ccomp`, `csubj`) asks, an `advcl` supposes. Frame, the tree's shape.]** Record: `202609180900_the-polar-question.md`.

22. **THE SUBJECT'S ROLE IS SETTLED BY WHAT IS PREDICATED OF IT, AND THE RULE IS ROWS** *(2026-09-18 — ten drill-gate witnesses, one question)* — `nsubj` is not a role: «the cat CHASED» is an agent, «I LOVE» an experiencer, «God EXISTS» a patient. **Copular adjectives separate perfectly** by the supersense of their related noun (WordNet files almost every adjective under `adj.all`, so the noun is read — *hungry* → *hunger*, `noun.state` → experiencer; *green* → *greenness*, `noun.attribute` → patient); a copular noun is a patient. **Verbs do not separate by any published instrument** — the supersense contradicted itself in the drill three times and VerbNet disagreed with E2's conventions on motion verbs — so the rule is a supersense table plus counted EXCEPTION ROWS (req 19's shape): `emotion`/`cognition`/`perception` → experiencer · `stative` → patient · otherwise agent · `disagree` → experiencer. **A lone `iobj` is abstained** — UD reserves it for the double-object clause, so «I trust YOU» labelled `iobj` is a provider mislabel and a recipient invented there would be wrongly-understood (req 8); a clausal complement counts as the other object. *Perception is a class by the Captain's ruling; «look», «watch», «listen» are acts and will need exception rows when a witness arrives. `develop` is a named miss: its role follows the SENSE, which the station never picks (req 11).* Knowledge, in `language_subject_roles` (`db/0018`), run by the marker selector's probes. **Known limit**: stimulus-subject psych verbs («the dog FRIGHTENS me» — `verb.emotion`, and the dog is no experiencer) will need exception rows when a witness arrives. Record: `202609181400_the-subject-role.md`.
