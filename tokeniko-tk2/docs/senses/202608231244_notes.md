## The mouth — the third non-dumb organ

The author's design: the brain sends to the senses **queue** a tkzip to say (based on reasoning and
intuition) — but **the actual creation of the final sentence is up to the mouth**, which closes with
rag-out, if enabled. A dedicated non-dumb part owns the actual form of the sentence expressed.

Three stages, one fence:

1. **render** — zip → faithful scaffold;
2. **color** — word choice within meaning-preserving bounds (the dictionary's synonym palette),
   steered by heart deviation + channel register + micro-nn;
3. **polish** — rag-out, when enabled.

The fence is the **round-trip check**: re-parse the final sentence, compare zips with the queued one;
drift in the WHAT → ship the scaffold verbatim. **The mouth styles, never restates.**

The author's extension, fully agreed: not just word swaps — **the articulation itself** (structure,
length, rhythm) is the mouth's. Anxiety clips sentences, joy expands them; behavior varies with the
channel AND with tokeniko's inner status. The zip bounds the meaning, the mouth owns ALL the surface
freedom — the fuzzy personality made audible: faithful yet never mechanical.
